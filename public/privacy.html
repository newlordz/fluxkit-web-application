Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

MasterfilePro provides browser-based file, document, QR code, writing, audio, and AI tools. This policy explains what information the service handles and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

When you create an account, we store your name, email address, password verifier, account status, and session records. If you sign in with Google, we receive your verified email address, name, and profile picture from Google. Google handles your Google password; MasterfilePro does not receive it.

Feedback and support messages may contain the contact details and text you choose to send. Please do not include passwords, payment card numbers, or sensitive documents in feedback.

Files and tool processing

Many MasterfilePro tools process files directly in your browser. Those files are not uploaded to our account database. When you choose a cloud AI, transcription, OCR, conversion, or other external-provider feature, the relevant file, extracted text, or prompt is sent to that provider so it can perform the requested operation. Provider use is subject to the provider's privacy and retention terms.

Only use cloud features with information you have the right to submit. Avoid submitting passwords, financial account credentials, medical records, or other highly sensitive information unless you understand and accept the provider's handling of it.

Sessions, security, and product insights

We use an opaque session token stored in your browser to keep you signed in. Account sessions normally expire after 30 days and can be revoked when you sign out or reset your password. Passwords are stored as salted, computationally expensive verifiers rather than plain text.

When product insights are enabled, the service may record a random visit identifier, the tool used, a funnel stage, and an event sequence. This is used to understand feature usage. It does not require us to inspect the contents of files processed locally in your browser.

Payments

If Pro payments are offered, payment details are handled by the payment provider. We receive payment status and transaction references needed to activate or reconcile your subscription; we do not store your full card details.

How information is used and shared

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To provide the tools, account features, support, and payment status you request.
  • To prevent abuse, investigate errors, and maintain service reliability.
  • To send essential account or security communications.
  • To service providers that host the database, process selected cloud-tool requests, or process payments.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users from fraud or abuse.

Retention

Account information is retained while your account is active. Expired or revoked session records may remain in the database for security and audit purposes. Provider-side processing and retention depend on the feature and provider you select. You can request account deletion by contacting us.

Your choices and rights

You can sign out, reset your password, request correction of account details, or request deletion of your account by emailing enochessel5@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, export, restrict, or object to certain processing. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Children

MasterfilePro is not directed to children under 13, and children under 13 should not create accounts or submit personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to enochessel5@gmail.com.
